### Scope and conventions

This note describes the arithmetic half of the development: the deduction of the Tate–Shafarevich length bound `UpperTheory.Top.VWin` from the Galois-representation statement `UpperTheory.GalStar.Row0b`, and the proof of `Row0b` itself. Every claim is tied to a Lean declaration. Sentences marked "(interpretation)" are readings of the formal statements and are not asserted by the code. No Lean code was compiled for this account; it rests on reading definitions and proof terms.

The top-level use is in `UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter_of_row`, which feeds both `Row0b` and `UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.recTExists` into the analytic half, together with `UpperTheory.One.vwin_of_row h : VWin`. The unconditional instance is `UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter : Row0b recT`, where `UpperTheory.Gal.Int.recT` is definitionally `fun h rc => UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.IsRecT h rc`, and `UpperTheory.V.Z.Win.RowT` is the same proposition.

### The target statement VWin

For a number field $E$, a prime $l$ and $e\in\mathbb Z$, an `Formalism.GaloisCohomology.LData` $D$ consists of the set $S$ of places of $E$ above $l$, chosen decomposition data at those places, and a character $\chi:G_{E,S}\to\mathbb Z_l^\times$ restricting to the cyclotomic character. Then `Formalism.GaloisCohomology.Sha` is
$$\text{Ш}^1(D,e)=\ker\Big(H^1\big(G_{E,S},(\mathbb Q_l/\mathbb Z_l)(\chi^{-e})\big)\to\prod_{v\mid l}H^1(G_v,\cdot)\Big),$$
and `shaLen` is its $\mathbb Z_l$-length in $\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\}$ (via `Formalism.GaloisCohomology.Sha1`, which intersects kernels of restriction maps). Note that $S$ consists only of places above $l$ (field `hS`).

`UpperTheory.Top.VVle E t B` says that for every algebraic closure and every family of `LData` (one for each prime), each $\text{Ш}^1(D_p,e)$ with $0<e<t$ has finite length, and for every finite set $S$ of primes
$$\sum_{l\in S}\log l\sum_{e=1}^{t-1}\operatorname{len}_{\mathbb Z_l}\text{Ш}^1(D_l,e)\le B.$$
`UpperTheory.Top.IsChiField ψ e q d E` says $E\subset\mathbb Q(\zeta_N)$ with $2^{e+2},\psi.N,q,d\mid N$ is the fixed field of $\{a: a\equiv\pm1 \bmod 2^{e+2},\ \psi(a)=1,\ (a/q)=(a/d)=1\}$. `UpperTheory.Top.VWin` asserts, for every primitive character $\psi$ (`PChar`), `VWinAt ψ 401`: for $0<\varepsilon<1/52$ and any $e(x)$ with $x^{2+8\varepsilon}\le 2^{e(x)}\le 2x^{2+8\varepsilon}$ (`PageN`), there are $J\ge1$ and $X_0$ such that for every `WinPair` $(q,d)$ at scale $X^{1/J}$ (primes $q\equiv3\bmod 8$, $d\equiv7\bmod16$, $\log q\asymp Y^{\varepsilon/5}$, $\log d\asymp Y^{\varepsilon/5}\log^2Y$, $q\mid d+1$ and every odd prime $\le Y^{\varepsilon/5}$ dividing $d+1$) one finds $Y\in[X^{1/J},X^{1/2}]$ with `VVle E ⌊x^401⌋ (√exp(x^ε))` for all $x\in[Y,Y^2]$ and all such $E$. Each such $E$ is CM (`UpperTheory.V.isCMField_of_isChiField`, invoked in `UpperTheory.V.vwinAt_of_joint`).

(interpretation) By Poitou–Tate (`Formalism.GaloisCohomology.PTShaAt`, `VFormula`) these lengths are tied to $H^2(G_{E,S},\mathbb Z_l(e+1))$, so the statement is a uniform logarithmic-height bound on étale cohomology groups of the kind that control $K$-groups and zeta values at negative integers of the fields in the family.

The proof term of `UpperTheory.One.vwin_of_row` is two lines: it calls `UpperTheory.One.Join.automorphic_window_of_inputs` with `OpenImageCondition.mk h`, the base change theorem `UpperTheory.BaseChangeIdentities.baseChangeN`, and `h`. The structure `UpperTheory.LiteratureTheorems.OpenImageCondition` has a single field of type `RowT`; the roughly sixty lemmas `UpperTheory.LiteratureTheorems.*_of_open` take it as an argument and ignore it, each being proved outright from other declarations (for instance `bernstein_zelevinsky_finiteness_of_open` is `PositivityTheory.Prw.bernsteinZelevinskyFiniteness_holds`). all in `UpperTheory.V.HV.*`, where `published_hypotheses_from_patching_alt` assembles `UpperTheory.V.Af.HypothesesH'` (rank-two Galois representations over totally real fields, multiplicity one, quaternionic and cyclic base change, descent).

### The plus/minus/2 decomposition and the source of 401 and $\sqrt{\exp(x^\varepsilon)}$

For CM $E$, `UpperTheory.V.vvle_of_parts` splits the sum: the $l=2$ part (`BoundedHeightTwoPlaceCondition`, bound $B_2$, weighted by $\log 2$), and for odd $l$ the complex-conjugation eigenspaces, `minusLen` and `plusLen` (`shaLen_eq_add`, via `length_eq_add_eigen`), giving
$$\text{VVle}\big(E,t,\;B_2\log2+B_-+B_+\big).$$
The three inputs, per `UpperTheory.V.vwinAt_of_joint`, are:

- the minus part, `UpperTheory.V.ThmBc` with `minusBound` $=3n t^2\log(tN)+C\sum_{l\mid o,\,l\ne2} l\,n^2t^2\log^2(tlN)$, proved via `ThmBcFree` from a Stickelberger-type bound (`UpperTheory.V.Odd.thmBcFree_of`, built from `stickBound` and `primeBound_of`);
- the $l=2$ part and the bad odd primes $l\in$ `Bchi ψ`, both from `UpperTheory.V.ThmBab`, whose `BaAt` and `BbAt` give bounds $\exp(x^{4\varepsilon/5})$, the latter for $\mathbb Z_l$-cyclotomic layers (`IsLayer`); its proof `thmBab_of_lemmaK` uses `UpperTheory.V.Published.FerreroWashington` (uniformly bounded $l$-rank of class groups up the cyclotomic $\mathbb Z_l$-tower of an abelian field containing $\mu_l$, the formal shape of $\mu=0$) and the crude `UpperTheory.V.LemmaK`, $\log\#H^2(\mathbb Z_l(e+1))\le\exp\big((2en)^{C}\log 2|d_L|\big)$;
- the plus part, `UpperTheory.V.ThmHV` with bound $(\mathrm{cond}(E)\,t)^C+C\exp(x^{4\varepsilon/5})$.

The final step `error_terms_le_sqrt_exp_rpow` absorbs everything into $\exp(x^\varepsilon/2)=\sqrt{\exp(x^\varepsilon)}$, since $4\varepsilon/5<\varepsilon$ and the conductor is $\exp(O(x^{\varepsilon/4}))$-bounded (`log_conductor_mul_height_le_of_pageN`). The constant 401 enters only as $t(x)=\lfloor x^{401}\rfloor$: `thmBab_of_lemmaK` gives `ThmBab K Ct` for every $C_t\ge1$, `thmHV_of_counts` for every $C_t>0$, and `thmBc_iff` shows `ThmBc Ct ↔ ThmBcFree` for $C_t\ge1$. (interpretation) The value 401 is therefore dictated by the analytic consumer of `VWin`; the arithmetic argument works for any polynomial range of twists.

### The plus part: character decomposition and the five cases

`UpperTheory.V.HV.pointHV_of_counts` reduces the plus part to Dirichlet characters $\psi$ of level $N=\mathrm{lcm}(2^{e+2},\psi.N)\,qd$ (`UpperTheory.V.HV.Host`) trivial on the subgroup `Host.H` with $\psi(-1)=(-1)^e$ (`Host.X`), and to the lengths `UpperTheory.V.HV.Vlen` $=\operatorname{len}\text{Ш}^1(G_{\mathbb Q},\,(K/\mathcal O)(\psi^{-1}\chi_{\rm cyc}^{-e}))$ with $S$ the primes dividing $lN$ (`UpperTheory.V.Chr.V'`, `Chr.rho`). The cases in `Host.CaseI`–`CaseZ` are: I, $l\notin B_\chi$ and $(l-1)\nmid 2e$; Z, $l\notin B_\chi$, $(l-1)\mid2e$, $\psi=1$; II, $e\ge2$, $\operatorname{ord}\chi_l(\psi)$ even and ($l\in B_\chi$ or $(l-1)\mid2e$); III, $l\notin B_\chi$, $(l-1)\mid 2e$, $\psi\ne1$ of odd order; IV, $l\in B_\chi$, $e\ge2$, odd order; V, $l\in B_\chi$, $e=1$. Here $B_\chi$ (`UpperTheory.V.Bchi`) is $\{3\}$ together with the odd primes dividing $\operatorname{ord}(\chi)\,\chi.N\,p_K h^*p_1$. `UpperTheory.V.HV.CountShape` demands $\operatorname{len}\cdot\log l\le(lNe)^C$ per character, while `CountI` demands the summed form $\sum_l\log l\sum_\psi\le(Nt)^C$.

The mechanism for `CountI` is visible in `UpperTheory.V.U3.PropGK`: for an auxiliary imaginary quadratic $K$ and prime $p$ there is a nonzero integer $N_N$ with $\log|N_N|\le c_5\,\varphi([\,\cdot\,])\,(N(e+1))^A\log p$ such that $\sum_\psi\operatorname{len}\le 2\,v_l(N_N)$ for every $l\ne p$. Summing $\log l\cdot v_l(N_N)$ over $l$ is at most $\log|N_N|$, which explains the $\log l$ weighting in `VVle`. The integer arises from `UpperTheory.V.Z.J.OneCharU`: with $\Psi=\chi_{\rm cyc}^{e}\psi$ and $t=\Psi(\sigma)+1+\Psi(\sigma)^{-1}$, the Selmer length is bounded by $2\sum_{\rho}v_l(\operatorname{tr}\rho(\sigma)-t)$ over admissible three-dimensional Galois representations $\rho$ of $G_K$ (`Adm3U`, `Adm2`), and the companion count statement in `AdmissibleUnitaryThreePoint.lean` bounds $\|N_N\|$ by the product of $\|\operatorname{tr}\rho(\mathrm{Frob}_w^{-1})-\mathrm{eis}_3\|$. (interpretation) This is an Eisenstein-congruence argument in the style of Ribet and Mazur–Wiles for $U(3)$ over $K$: Selmer classes of $\Psi$ are bounded by congruences between automorphic three-dimensional representations and the Eisenstein triple $\Psi\oplus1\oplus\Psi^{-1}$, and the congruence ideal divides an explicit nonzero integer. The commutative-algebra step bounding a Selmer length by a cotangent length is `UpperTheory.V.Alg.M.main_holds` and `mprime_holds` (`LemmaM`, `LemmaM'`).

Kato's Euler system appears as `UpperTheory.V.Z.Kato.WeilSphericalRecords.KatoTwoW` and `UpperTheory.V.U3.KatoUse`: for a newform of weight $2e+1$ attached to $W\otimes\chi_{\rm cyc}^{-e}$, the Bloch–Kato Selmer groups (`UpperTheory.V.Af.BlochKatoSelmerVanishes`) of $W$ and $W\otimes\theta$ vanish; it is proved in `katoTwoW_of_printsH` from `HypothesesH'`. Cases II and III use $\mathrm{GSp}_4$: `UpperTheory.V.Aut.SymplecticFourImageDense` asserts irreducibility and automorphy of four-dimensional data in each case, combined in `UpperTheory.V.Th.sp4Main_alt` with a rigidity input and `numberSp4_n_alt`. Case V uses a reduced unitary lift (`UpperTheory.V.UnitaryThreeInertia.countV_of_finiteness_duality_euler_host`), and case IV and the large-residue subcase are absorbed into `ThmBab` via `countIV` and `countVs`.

### Threshold arguments and dense threshold cases

The directory `ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases` is the assembly layer for the above. Its central formal statements are `UpperTheory.V.Th.AdjointLiftingTheorem` and `UpperTheory.V.Th.DenseThresholdMainCase`: for a CM field $L$ without $\mu_l$, an ordinary Hida setting, $2\le n\le4$, a residual representation and its universal ordinary determinant ring, every locally generic automorphic point $x$ with Galois representation $\rho$ (`AutomorphicPointLocallyGeneric`: continuous, arithmetic, conjugate self-dual automorphic, unramified at the auxiliary set, with ordinary flags) has vanishing adjoint Selmer group (`AdZero`, `OrdZero`). (interpretation) This corresponds to adjoint Selmer vanishing at automorphic points of Hida families, in the style of results of Allen and of Newton–Thorne, here proved inside the development (`UpperTheory.V.Th.adjoint_lifting_of_cohomology`) and then used to make Selmer groups of characters, embedded in adjoint Selmer groups of the reducible residual representations, small.

### Proof of Row0b: statement

`UpperTheory.GalStar.Row0b Rc` says: for every CM field $E$, prime $l$, $\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_l\cong\mathbb C$, $a\ge1$, cuspidal $\pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_a(\mathbb A_E)$ which is regular algebraic (`IsRegAlg`) and polarized (`GlobalTheory.ContraPair.PolarizedPair π χ`), there exist $r:G_E\to\mathrm{GL}_a(\overline{\mathbb Q}_l)$, $wt$, and Hodge–Tate data $HT$ with `PropsB`: $r$ continuous and semisimple; Hodge–Tate multisets equal $\{(a-1)/2-z\}$ for the infinitesimal character and satisfy $HT(\tau c)=wt-HT(\tau)$; `away`; and `above`.

`AwayB x y` (for $x$ the Weil–Deligne representation of $r$ at $w\nmid l$ and $y=\mathrm{rc}(\pi_w)$ for every `rc` with `Rc`) contains three clauses: $\dim x=\dim y$; if $y$ has abelian Weil image (`Tor`) and $N=0$ then $x=y$; and if $E_w\cong\mathbb Q_g$ with $a=1$ or $g>16a^2$ (`IsQpBig`), $y$ is tame (`TmW`), square-free in the sense of `Sqf`, and $N_y=0$, then $N_x=0$ and $\operatorname{charpol}_x(g)=\operatorname{charpol}_y(g)$ for all $g$ of degree $0$, that is, on inertia. `AboveB` asserts crystallinity, de Rham and the Hodge–Tate jumps at $w\mid l$ when $\pi_w$ is spherical, and de Rham, potential semistability, jumps and the same `AwayB` relation for the $D_{\rm pst}$ Weil–Deligne representation when $\mathrm{rc}(\pi_w)$ falls into the tame or toral case with $N=0$.

### Proof of Row0b: from geometry and theta lifts

The proof term is `galoisRepresentations_of_thetaLift UpperTheory.TP.K.tLiftK`, which unfolds to `UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisLocalGlobalStrong_of_geometry_lift geoOne_holds tLiftK`, in turn `Row0.row0b` applied to
$$\texttt{rest}\big(\texttt{coreAll\_of}(\texttt{seedFinOdd\_of\_geoOne}\ geo,\ \texttt{congFinOdd\_of\_ex}\ \texttt{wtLacEx\_holds},\ up)\big)$$
in `IntegralStructures/Development.lean`. The steps are as follows.

1. Geometric seed. `UpperTheory.Gal.Int.Geo.GeoOne` takes odd $a$, a CM field containing $\sqrt{-d}$, an automorphic $U$ on the unitary group $U(a)$ of type `GlobalTheory.CongW.TyEll` at a place $w_0\mid l$ (a character of order $l^m$ of a torsion-free compact open $J$ whose isotypic vectors force supercuspidality) and returns representations $\rho$ with matching Frobenius eigenvalues (`ESAt`), crystallinity and Hodge–Tate jumps at good places above $l$. The representations are matrix forms of Hecke-eigenpieces of total $l$-adic cohomology of a smooth projective tower with finite étale correspondences (`HTower`, `Nice`, `piece`, `IsMat`), up to passage to a graded piece of a block-triangular form (`IsGr`). `weight_one_geometric_realization` supplies `HolomorphicLefschetzFormula`: for a finite étale correspondence $(c_1,c_2)$ on a smooth projective complex $Z$ with nondegenerate fixed points $y$ and tangent maps $A_y$,
$$\sum_b(-1)^b\operatorname{tr}\big(c\mid H^b(Z,\Omega^a)\big)=\sum_y\frac{\operatorname{tr}\wedge^aA_y}{\det(1-A_y)},$$
proved in `UpperTheory.Cb.lefschetz_fixedPoint_finite_etale_correspondence`; it is used in `kodaira_spencer_of_weight_one` (`KSHodge`) to show the required Hodge classes occur. (interpretation) This is the Atiyah–Bott holomorphic Lefschetz formula, used to locate Hodge–Tate weights in the cohomology of Kuga–Sato type families (`GlobalTheory.KS.pow`) over compact models (`ZPr`).

2. Seed to limit. `GlobalTheory.CongL.SeedFinOdd` (= `SeedWU4 Odd TyEll …`) gives Galois representations for type-`TyEll` forms with Frobenius characteristic polynomials matching Satake parameters at Frobenius elements lying in an open dense $V\subset G_E$. `CongFinOdd` (= `CongUW4`) approximates the Hecke eigensystem of an arbitrary $U$ $l$-adically by eigensystems of `TyEll` forms at a fixed level (`PolyApprox`), and `UpperTheory.Gal.Lim.limFinOdd_of` combines them into `LimFinOdd`: for odd $a$, a continuous semisimple $r$ with `FrobEig` at all unramified split places, crystalline/de Rham clauses and a joint approximation of Frobenius traces at $l$ (`JointApprox`). (interpretation) This corresponds to the congruence-and-limit construction of Galois representations by interpolating traces, as in Chenevier–Harris and Sorensen, with a supercuspidal type in place of eigenvarieties.

3. Parity via theta lifting. `UpperTheory.TP.TLiftK` asserts that for $d\ge d_0$, every automorphic $U$ of $U(n)$ with weights $\lambda$ satisfying $d+m_W\le2\lambda_k$ has a theta lift $U'$ to $U(d)$ (`IsLift`), relative to Hecke characters $\chi_X,\chi_W$; it is proved by `tLift_holds_k` from a Siegel–Weil formula and doubling-method package (`tools_holds_k`). Through `UpperTheory.Gal.TP.twoUpOK_of_k` it yields `Sub.TwoUp`: an even-rank $U$ lifts to two odd-rank forms $U_1,U_2$ whose Satake parameters are those of $U\otimes\kappa^{-1}$ together with disjoint sets of characters $\{\psi_{1,i}\}$, $\{\psi_{2,j}\}$. `Subtract` and `rowDef_of_odd` then recover the even-rank Galois representation by removing the characters. (interpretation) This is a version of the standard device of adding characters to change parity; it corresponds to stable-range theta correspondence for unitary dual pairs (Kudla–Rallis, Howe duality only implicitly).

4. Core to CoreAll. `UpperTheory.Gal.Int.Core.toCoreK` produces `GlobalTheory.RoadU.Core`, existence of weakly attached $r$ (`GlobalTheory.GalCut.Weak`: continuity and almost-everywhere Frobenius compatibility, `AttC`) plus `SphericalLevelCondition` for every weakly attached $r$, at level `.csd` (CM fields with an imaginary quadratic subfield, $[E^+:\mathbb Q]$ even, controlled non-split places, $\chi=\omega^a$). `coreAll_of_core` extends to all CM fields and all polarizations using descent (`UpperTheory.Gal.Desc.descOffT`) and closed-Phi functoriality (`congPhi_holds`). Base change `PositivityTheory.Rg.BcN.BaseChangeIsobaricN`, proved as `UpperTheory.BaseChangeIdentities.baseChangeN`, asserts that for CM $E$ with $E/E^+$ unramified at all finite places, every automorphic $\pi$ on $U(n)$ has an isobaric weak base change $\boxplus V_i$ to $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb A_E)$ with conjugate self-dual discrete $V_i$ and compatibility at split and unramified inert places (`IsBaseChangeN`). (interpretation) This corresponds to the stable base change of Labesse and Mok in the form needed here.

5. Local–global compatibility. `UpperTheory.Gal.Int.Row.galoisLocalGlobal_of_localGlobalCompatibility` derives `Row0` from `CoreAll`, the toral statement `R1` (`galoisCompat_unlinkedSupport_of_spherical`: unlinked principal-series support) and `LocalGlobalCompatAtIwahoriFixedPlaces`. The place-class statement it uses requires $\pi_w$ to have $K_1$-fixed vectors, $E_w\cong\mathbb Q_g$ and $N=0$, and concludes inertia characteristic polynomial equality away from $l$ and potential semistability with the same inertia data above $l$.

### Local Langlands: recT and LawsT

`UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.IsRecT h rc` means there is a local Artin map $a$ with `LawsT a ha rc`: dimension preservation, compatibility with characters, Langlands multisegment classification `rc x = segSum rc l` (Zelevinsky segments), irreducibility on cuspidals, compatibility with $|\det|^s$ twists, absolute value of the central character, and, only for `Good` $x$ (supercuspidal support consisting of characters, or over $\mathbb Q_g$ of twists of depth-zero cuspidals), twisting, central character, duality, injectivity and tameness. `TypeLaw` is Green's formula: if a depth-zero $\sigma$ inflates a cuspidal $\tau$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb F_{g^n})$ attached to a regular character $\theta$ with $\tau(y)=(-1)^{n-1}\sum_{j<n}\theta(z^{g^j})$ on elliptic regular $y$ with eigenvalue $z$, then inertia acts through $\prod_j(X-\theta(z^{g^j}))$.

`UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.recTExists` builds `rc` from `cuspEx` and Zelevinsky extension `exists_isLExt`. `cuspEx` defines `rc` on cuspidals by `if GoodCusp x then r₀ x else (J x.1).lpTw (e x)`, where `r₀` is the depth-zero parametrization (`goodMap`, `Par`) and `J` is a fixed irreducible representation of the right dimension supplied by `UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.junkEx`, chosen over $\mathbb Q_g$ to differ from every twist of a tame representation. `RecTUnique` only asserts uniqueness on `Good` representations. (interpretation) Thus `recT` is a partial local Langlands correspondence, genuine on the depth-zero and principal-series part, with an arbitrary placeholder elsewhere; since `Row0b` quantifies over every `rc` satisfying `IsRecT`, its local conditions carry content only where `LawsT` pins `rc` down, which matches the `IsQpBig`, tame, toral and $N=0$ hypotheses in `AwayB` and `AboveB`.

### Relation to the literature

Where the formal statement matches: `Row0b` corresponds to a weakened form of the theorems of Harris–Taylor, Shin and Chenevier–Harris on Galois representations for regular algebraic polarized cuspidal representations of $\mathrm{GL}_a$ over CM fields; its local–global clauses correspond to special cases of the compatibility of Caraiani and Barnet-Lamb–Gee–Geraghty–Taylor (unramified and principal-series components, depth-zero inertia at large degree-one places). `FerreroWashington` corresponds to the Ferrero–Washington theorem. `KatoTwoW` corresponds to consequences of Kato's Euler system. `BlochKatoSelmerVanishes` corresponds to the Bloch–Kato Selmer condition. The local Langlands content corresponds to Harris–Taylor and Henniart only on depth-zero and principal-series representations.

### Points for a referee

1. `vwin_of_row` and `automorphic_window_of_inputs` are assembly lemmas; `OpenImageCondition` contains only `RowT`, and every `*_of_open` lemma ignores it. The "literature theorems" (Ferrero–Washington, Poitou–Tate, Kato, Siegel–Weil, Bernstein–Zelevinsky, base change) are internal proofs whose statements should each be checked against the published theorems.
2. The name `galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter` overstates `Row0b`: `AwayB` gives full equality only for abelian $N=0$ parameters and inertia equality only in the tame, square-free, $N=0$ case at places with $E_w\cong\mathbb Q_g$, $g>16a^2$; `AboveB` gives de Rham and Hodge–Tate information only at spherical places or in those cases; no irreducibility is claimed.
3. `recT` uses a placeholder `junkEx` on non-good supercuspidals; `LawsT` and `RecTUnique` constrain only `Good` representations.
4. `SeedWU4` matches Frobenius polynomials only on an open dense $V$; the passage to `FrobEig` at every unramified split place in `limWU4_of` deserves inspection.
5. Geometric seeds are graded pieces (`IsGr`) of Hecke eigenspaces; `HolomorphicLefschetzFormula` is a major theorem proved in about 360 lines plus dependencies.
6. `TLiftK`, `BaseChangeIsobaricN` and `TwoUpOK` carry hypotheses ($E/E^+$ unramified everywhere, $\sqrt{-d}\in E$, $[E^+:\mathbb Q]$ even, `InertOK`) whose removal in `coreAll_of_core` should be traced.
7. The constant 401 is imposed by the consumer; the arithmetic theorems hold for any $C_t\ge1$. `LemmaK` is doubly exponential and enters only through `ThmBab`.
8. `VVle` quantifies over all `LData`, with $S$ the places above $l$ only; independence of choices is a separate proposition (`Formalism.GaloisCohomology.Indep`).
